# 🌐 SDG Quest - 10-Level Sustainability Challenge

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-emerald.svg)](LICENSE)
[![UN SDGs](https://img.shields.io/badge/UN-17%20SDGs-00689D.svg)](https://sdgs.un.org/goals)
[![GitHub Pages](https://img.shields.io/badge/Hosted%20with-GitHub%20Pages-blue.svg)](https://pages.github.com/)

**SDG Quest** is an interactive, browser-based educational game created to evaluate, challenge, and expand public knowledge regarding the **United Nations 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**.

Players register their profile, navigate through 10 dynamic levels, inspect global SDG indicators via an interactive world map, unlock achievement badges, and receive a personalized UN SDG Certificate of Accomplishment upon completion.

## ✨ Key Features & Interactive Mechanics

* 👤 **Player Registration & Personalization**: Input your name and school/organization to receive personalized status metrics, real-time response feedback, and an official SDG Certificate.
* 🗺️ **Interactive Global SDG Index Map**: Explore regional SDG progress ratings, data insights, and regional indexes across North America, Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa, Asia & Pacific, and Latin America.
* 💡 **Level-by-Level SDG Fact Files**: Real-time side panel updating with UN data points, statistical metrics, and key performance indicators per level.
* 🎯 **Diverse Game Mechanics**:
  * **Multiple Choice**: Foundational UN baseline metrics and poverty targets.
  * **Policy Scenarios**: Allocate development resources for maximum compound impact.
  * **True or False / Myth vs. Fact**: Debunk common misconceptions regarding climate, energy costs, and global ocean dynamics.
  * **Drag-and-Drop Matching**: Pair water scarcity indicators directly to global sanitation definitions.
* 🔊 **Native Web Audio Effects**: Integrated Web Audio API tone synthesizer providing instant sound cues for right/wrong answers and victory fanfare (no external MP3 assets needed).
* 🏆 **Dynamic Certificate & Confetti Showcase**: High-score tracking, badge gallery, and celebratory confetti effects powered by Canvas Confetti.

| Level | Goal Focus | SDG Alignment | Game Mechanic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Poverty & Hunger | **SDG 1** (No Poverty) & **SDG 2** (Zero Hunger) | Multiple Choice |
| **Level 2** | Health & Well-being | **SDG 3** (Good Health & Well-Being) | True / False |
| **Level 3** | Education & Gender Equality | **SDG 4** (Quality Education) & **SDG 5** (Gender Equality) | Policy Scenario |
| **Level 4** | Clean Water & Sanitation | **SDG 6** (Clean Water & Sanitation) | Drag-and-Drop Matching |
| **Level 5** | Affordable & Clean Energy | **SDG 7** (Affordable & Clean Energy) | Myth vs. Fact |
| **Level 6** | Decent Work & Economic Growth | **SDG 8** (Decent Work & Economic Growth) | Multiple Choice |
| **Level 7** | Industry & Innovation | **SDG 9** (Industry & Innovation) & **SDG 10** (Reduced Inequalities) | Multiple Choice |
| **Level 8** | Sustainable Cities | **SDG 11** (Sustainable Cities) & **SDG 12** (Responsible Consumption) | Policy Scenario |
| **Level 9** | Climate Action & Oceans | **SDG 13** (Climate Action) & **SDG 14** (Life Below Water) | True / False |
| **Level 10**| Life on Land & Partnerships | **SDG 15** (Life on Land) & **SDG 17** (Partnerships for the Goals) | Multiple Choice |
